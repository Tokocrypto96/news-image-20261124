The front page of **THE DAILY GLOBE** for **Tuesday, 24 November 2026** — one image
summing up the day's world news. Lead story: Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as
CEO and the stock soars 31%, adding almost $1 trillion. Also inside: the COP31
climate talks collapse in Antalya, FIFA names the 2031 and 2035 Women's World
Cup hosts, the Russian cargo ship Sparta II is towed home after a drone strike,
24 die in an Indonesian factory fire, and Gukesh begins his world chess title
defence — plus an IN BRIEF strip. Every story was reported 23–24 November 2026
(Reuters, CNN, FIFA, AP).

The image is drawn by `render.py` (Python + Pillow) and shipped as `entry.png`;
`main.py` (stdlib only) copies it to the output directory.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

This writes `out/entry.png` (1024×1024 PNG, 155,136 bytes). Running it twice
produces byte-identical output.
